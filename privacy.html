Privacy Policy: AND NEW

  • Effective date: 12 November 2026
  • Developer: Kamronbek Kamchiev (individual), Uzbekistan
  • Contact: tobyuz2107@gmail.com

AND NEW ("the app", "we") is an MMA draft game with online rankings. It has no sign-up, no email or password, no ads and no purchases. This policy explains the little data the rankings need, where it's kept, and how to delete it.

1. What stays on your device

The app saves your game on your device: your display name, a random player number, your careers, drafts, scores, 1 vs 1 history and Guess the fighter results. Deleting the app removes all of it.

2. What we collect for the rankings

When the app is online, it creates an anonymous player for you on our server. There is no account: you never give an email, phone number or password. We collect:

  • A random player ID created by the server, and a login token that keeps your phone signed in to that player.
  • Your display name, if you set one (otherwise you appear as "Rookie" plus a few characters of your player ID).
  • Your ranked careers: the mode, the moves of your draft, the result, the score and when you played. The server replays each career to check it before it counts.
  • Your daily Guess the fighter results: the puzzle number, whether you solved it, how many clues you opened and your points.
  • Name reports: if you report a name, which player you reported and the name at the time.

Our hosting provider also processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit abuse (for example, how many new players one address can create per hour). We don't store it with your game data.

What other players see: your display name and your points on the rankings. Nothing else about you is shown.

3. Why we use it

Only to run the rankings: to show the boards, to check careers so nobody can fake a score, to show how many players solved today's puzzle, and to hide offensive names. We don't use it for advertising, we don't sell or share it, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

4. Where it's kept

The rankings run on Supabase (supabase.com), which hosts our database in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Supabase processes the data for us as a service provider. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

5. How long we keep it

Until you delete it, or until we close the rankings. Unused draft tickets are removed automatically.

6. Delete your data

Open Menu › Delete my data in the app. It deletes your player, name, careers, Guess the fighter results and reports from our server, then clears the game on your phone. You need to be online for this. You can also email tobyuz2107@gmail.com from anywhere and we'll delete it for you; tell us the player ID shown on your My Stats screen.

7. Names on the rankings

Names are checked against a list of offensive words. Players can report a name by pressing and holding it on the rankings. A name reported by several players is hidden, and we may also hide or rename names that break these rules.

8. Sharing

When you tap Share on a result, a Guess the fighter round or Share my code in 1 vs 1, the app opens your device's share sheet with a message you can see before sending. Where it goes is your choice, and the app you share to has its own privacy policy. A 1 vs 1 fight is worked out on your device from the code you paste.

9. What we don't do

  • No email, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • No ads, no advertising ID and no tracking.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own. Apple may share crash reports with us if you have turned on sharing with app developers in your iPhone's settings; these don't identify you.

10. Children

AND NEW is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the rankings, email us and we'll delete the data.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK), you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Use Menu › Delete my data, or email tobyuz2107@gmail.com with your player ID. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Changes

We'll update this policy before collecting anything new, for example when ads or subscriptions arrive. We'll change the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
