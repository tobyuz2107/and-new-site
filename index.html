Delete my data

Open Menu › Delete my data while online. It removes your name and scores from the rankings and clears the game on your phone. Or email us with the player ID from your My Stats screen. See the Privacy Policy.

Why didn't my career count for the rankings?

Careers started without an internet connection are practice runs. Start your next one online and it counts; if you finish it offline, it's sent the next time you open the app online.

Someone has an offensive name

Press and hold the name on the Rankings tab and choose Report. Names reported by several players are hidden.

A fighter's record or rating looks wrong

Records come from public sources and ratings are our own opinion for the game. Email us and we'll take a look.
